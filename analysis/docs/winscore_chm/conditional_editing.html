CONDITIONAL EDITING.

Many parametric changes may be made at once with this editing system whose syntax is similar to the BASIC programming language. The main components of the SCORE edit language are IF, AND, OR and THEN. When a set of conditions are found to be true, then parameters may be changed, items may be deleted, or items may be selected to be "cut" for pasting elsewhere later. Color or gray scale parameters may also be set. (See Color Control.)

 

The editing statements are comprised of two main parts; the IF section and the THEN section. If the IF section is .TRUE. then the changes of the THEN section take place. The semicolon (;) may be typed in place of THEN. 

The legal elements of the IF section include:

 

     Pn where n is any parameter number. 

     =                       equal. 

     <> or ~ (tilde)   not equal. 

     <                       less than.  

     >                       greater than. 

     <= or {              less than or equal. 

     >= or }              greater than or equal. 

     +                       plus. 

     -                        minus. 

     *                       multiply. 

     /                        divide.

 

Numbers may have up to 4 digits to the left of the decimal point and up to 3 digits to the right. Left and right parenthese may be used to define statements which include AND or OR. MOD(Pn,x) gives the remainder result of dividing the number "x" into the value in Pn. (see example)

 

The legal elements of the THEN section include:

 

     Pn where n is any parameter number. 

     =               equal. 

     +               plus.  

     -                minus. 

     *               multiply. 

     /                divide. 

     , (comma)  separates each parameter change after THEN. 

     Any number (as above). 

     DEL         delete the items. 

     CUT         cut the items for later pasting (they will not be deleted). 

     RGB n =  the color number (up to 6 digits) 

     GRA n =  the gray scale number (from 0 to .99)  

     BLA         changes color or gray items to normal black.

 
EXAMPLES:
   IF P3 > 100 THEN P3=P3-20  (or IF P3 > 100 ; P3=p3-20)
      This will move all items, on all staves, which are beyond position 100, 20 steps to the right.
   IF P3 > 100 AND (P1=9 OR P1=18) THEN CUT
      This will "cut" items with code numbers 9 or 18 which are to right of position 100.
   IF (P1=1 AND P2<4) OR (P1=2 AND P2>=6) THEN DEL
      This will delete all notes on staves lower than 4 and all rests on staves greater than or equal to 6.
   IF(P1=1 AND P2>3 AND MOD(P9,10) <> 0) OR (P1=1 AND P2=1 AND P3>100) THEN P11=5, P6=6
      If either of the strings enclosed by parentheses are .TRUE., then the note will have an
      accent (P11=5) and the note head will be an X (P6=6). The 1st conditional requires that the
      code number be 1 and that the note is on a staff higher than 3. The MOD statement means
      that the note must have a tail. (i.e. the remainder of P9/10 must not equal 0.) The 2nd
      statement requires that the note must be on staff 1 and to the right of position 100.
      Nested parentheses are not supported (aside from use with MOD).

      The current state of the screen is saved to !SAVE.MUS just before the IF command 
        is executed.
